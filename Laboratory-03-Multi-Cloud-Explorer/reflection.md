# Mission Reflection

Among AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform, Google Cloud impressed me the most because of its strong capabilities in artificial intelligence, machine learning, data, and Kubernetes. I found it interesting how cloud platforms can provide different services for different types of workloads and business needs.

AWS, Azure, and GCP have many similarities. All three provide computing, storage, networking, databases, identity and security services, and tools for managing cloud resources. They also use global infrastructure with regions and other infrastructure locations to help organizations deploy applications closer to their users.

Business requirements have a major influence on cloud platform selection. A company needs to consider its budget, existing technologies, location, workload, scalability, security, and technical requirements before choosing a provider. For example, a company that already depends heavily on Microsoft technologies may benefit from Azure, while an AI-focused organization may prefer Google Cloud.

Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant taught me that choosing a cloud provider is not simply about choosing the most popular platform. The best choice depends on the specific problem and requirements of the client. I learned how to compare services and connect technical features with actual business needs.

My GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio has improved because I added a new laboratory activity with research about three major cloud providers, comparison tables, client recommendations, screenshots, and a reflection. This makes my portfolio more organized and shows my progress in learning cloud computing.
